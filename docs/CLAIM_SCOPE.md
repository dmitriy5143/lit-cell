# Claim Scope

## Supported

- The method is causal under the stated online issue/update protocol.
- Completed innovations have spatially local, time-specific, and
  identity-specific predictive content.
- Bounded local transport improves cumulative h6 error on MDCK Bulk, MDCK Edge,
  HUVEC, and MDA-MB-231 under the reported domain-specific protocols.
- The sparse transport is practically equivalent to the dense reference within
  the registered tolerance and scales near-linearly in the measured regime.
- A compact E(2)-equivariant operator explains most of the dense correction's
  gain on MDCK and survives wrong-cell and stale-time controls.
- Student-t and fold-external calibration provide useful uncertainty estimates.

## Supported with Qualifications

- The cumulative MDCK Bulk h6 effect is large and positive in 6/6 development
  movies, but the registered two-hypothesis Holm-adjusted value is 0.0625.
- Movies 10-16 confirm the frozen configuration, but are not described as a
  completely prospective untouched cohort.
- HUVEC and MDA-MB-231 show reproducibility of the training procedure within
  each domain; they are not zero-shot transfer of all learned weights.
- DeepSea is a partial cross-dataset transfer. LifeAct has only three independent
  conditions and supports an uncertainty-scale signal, not a mean correction.

## Not Supported

- global state of the art across pedestrian, vehicle, or all cell-trajectory tasks;
- a proven h1 improvement over every matched comparator;
- equivalence between streaming h6 and open-loop six-step forecasting;
- physical identification of force, traction, stress, or thermodynamic energy;
- a causal conditional-mean benefit from raw video, masks, morphology, or direct
  mechanical measurements in the final LaChance model;
- use of a fixed-origin candidate oracle as a lower bound for streaming h6.

## Recommended One-Sentence Positioning

> A compact causal online architecture uses a learned individual state and a
> bounded graph transport of already completed neighbour innovations to reduce
> cumulative cell-motion error across four cellular domains, while controlled
> field analysis separates robust kinematic structure from unverified mechanics.
